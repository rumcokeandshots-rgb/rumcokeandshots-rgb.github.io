Delete your Agoneld account

To delete your account and all data tied to it:

  1. Open Agoneld and sign in.
  2. Open Account on the main screen and tap Delete account.
  3. Type your password and tap Delete forever.

This permanently removes your account, progress, wallet, items, market orders and chat messages from our server. Progress stored only on your device is removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling.

Can't sign in? Email rumcokeandshots@gmail.com from any address with your player name and we will delete the account.
