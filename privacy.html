Agoneld Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. Contact: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com

What we collect

  • Game progress on your device (characters, items, settings), stored in your device storage.
  • If you create an account: your player name and a password stored only as a salted hash, plus your game progress, wallet, items and Auction House listings.
  • Chat messages you send in Global or Trade chat, with your player name and time. Messages are visible to other players and may be reviewed if reported.
  • Technical data such as your IP address, used only to limit abuse.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads (for example to revive your character after you fall) using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice from the "Ad privacy options" link in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, email address, precise location, contacts or photos, and we do not sell your personal data.

How it is used

To run the game, keep your progress across devices, run the Auction House and chat, show ads, and prevent abuse.

Purchases

If real-money purchases (Diamonds) are offered, payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

Agoneld is not directed at children under 13. Please do not share personal information in chat.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. You can delete it any time in the game: Account, then Delete account, or use the account deletion page. Chat messages and market orders from the account are removed with it.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
